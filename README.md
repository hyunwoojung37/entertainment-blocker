# Entertainment App/Web Blocker
## Purpose and Motivation

Its a lock that prevents you from using entertainment websites (later will be implemented on apps) that you can personally categorize whether if they are considered an entertainment app to you or not. To bypass this lock, you have to submit any work that you did and show an AI that will determine if you rushed your work or look for quality mistakes to ensure that you complete your work. 

I personally created this because I have a hard time focussing on my work especially when entertainment is so easily accessible, so this will help me and hopefully you focus on your work and instill discipline and make you feel like you actually did work after you did your assignments.

## Features & Goals

1. Personally categorized entertainment apps or webs to block
2. AI reviewed work to ensure its not slop submitted
3. Restricts usage of apps to increase productivity
